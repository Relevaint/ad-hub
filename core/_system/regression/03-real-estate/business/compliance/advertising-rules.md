# Advertising rules

These strings must appear exactly as written. Do not shorten or reword them.

- Brokerage name in every ad: **Harbor Pine Realty LLC**
- License line on every landing page and in every ad that has room for it: **Licensed real estate broker, license RB-000000**
- Equal housing line: **Equal Housing Opportunity**

Rules from our broker of record:

- Never describe a neighborhood or a buyer by who lives there or who they are: family
  status, age, religion, race, national origin, disability or sex.
- Never promise a sale price or a time to sell.
- Testimonials only from real clients who signed our release form.
