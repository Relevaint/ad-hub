# Pipeline

CRM stages for a home value lead, with typical timing and conversion (last 18 months):

| Stage | Typical time after the form | Share of the previous stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New lead | same day | |
| Contacted | 1 day | 85% |
| Appointment set | 2 to 7 days | 15% of leads |
| Listing signed | 2 to 6 weeks | 50% of appointments |
| Under contract | 1 to 3 months | 70% of listings |
| Closed | 3 to 6 months after the form | 85% of contracts |

Average gross commission per closed side: $9,400. The brokerage keeps 30% after the
agent split.
