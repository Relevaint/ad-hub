# Clearflow Gutters

<!-- Synthetic regression fixture for Ad-Hub. Not a real business. -->

Gutter cleaning and small gutter repairs for houses. Family run, 11 years in business,
fully insured. Two crews.

## Where we work

The twelve towns of Millbrook County: Ashby, Belden, Carrow, Dunmore, Elston, Fairhaven,
Greyford, Hollins, Ivers, Juniper Falls, Kettle Hill and Lowmoor. We do not work in
Eastport across the river; the bridge tolls and traffic make it lose money.

## How people book

Website form or phone. The form asks for address and roof height. Everything goes into
our CRM. See `notes/crm-pipeline.md`.

## Busy seasons

Fall is everything. From October to late November both crews are usually full by the
middle of October. Spring is the second, smaller rush.
