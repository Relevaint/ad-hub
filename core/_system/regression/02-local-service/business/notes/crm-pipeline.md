# CRM pipeline

Stages, and how long it usually takes to reach each one (from the last 12 months):

| Stage | Typical time after the form | Share of leads that reach it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New lead | same day | 100% |
| Estimate booked | within 1 day | 60% |
| Estimate done | 2 to 4 days | 55% |
| Job won | 5 to 10 days | 27% |
| Job done | 1 to 3 weeks | 26% |

About 14 leads a month outside the fall rush. Many raw leads are outside our area or
want roof work we do not do, which is why we count from "Estimate booked".

Each lead record has a "source" field. Ad leads carry the ad's tracking tag when they
come through the form. Phone leads do not.
