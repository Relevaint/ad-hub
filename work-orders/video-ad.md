## Needs

- Required: the offer in a sentence, and who it is for
- Helps, never blocks: a planned ledger row; the proof table; supplied footage; a connected video tool

## Layers

### 1. Fundamentals (`fundamentals`)

- **Build:** A compact brief: audience and situation, awareness and surface, one desire or problem, the offer and its defensible benefit, proof available and claims forbidden, one message and action, constraints, the exact deliverables asked for; plus a source note for the destination page and brand material actually looked at.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md, "pass 1 fundamentals before creative"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md#pass-1-fundamentals-before-creative); [ad-creative/SKILL.md, "1 take the brief from the row"](../core/skills/ad-creative/SKILL.md#1-take-the-brief-from-the-row)
- **Check:** Can this ad do one clear job for this audience inside the brief, with every unknown fact left unknown?
- **Evidence:** The offer file's proof table and limits; the destination page opened and viewed, or the gap named; the ledger row's sentence when there is one.
- **If it fails:** Resolve the brief or ask only for the blocking choice. Never invent a customer story or a fact to move on.

### 2. Premises (`premises`)

- **Build:** Several materially different premises, one sentence each, with why this audience would care and why the product belongs in the resolution.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md, "pass 2 premises not polished headlines"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md#pass-2-premises-not-polished-headlines); [ad-creative/references/opening-dynamics.md, "the table awareness decides the opening"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/opening-dynamics.md#the-table-awareness-decides-the-opening)
- **Check:** Do the candidates differ in the underlying idea rather than the wording, and does each have a payoff the product is part of?
- **Evidence:** The premises side by side, with the ones rejected and why.
- **If it fails:** Write new premises here. A category slogan or a novelty with no product payoff is rejected, not polished.

### 3. Cast and treatment (`cast-treatment`)

- **Build:** For the strongest premises: who or what acts, and separately how it is rendered, with the materially different alternative that was rejected.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md, "pass 3 cast and treatment are independent decisions"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md#pass-3-cast-and-treatment-are-independent-decisions)
- **Check:** Were cast and rendering chosen as two separate decisions, each for a stated reason tied to the brief and the brand evidence, not by default?
- **Evidence:** The chosen cast and rendering, the alternative considered, and the reason for each.
- **If it fails:** A default choice returns to treatment exploration here. A cast that changes the idea's logic returns to premises.

### 4. Visual stop and hierarchy (`visual-stop`)

- **Build:** The actual first frame or single image, described concretely: focal point, action or contrast, what is understood at once, the question it raises; then its hierarchy: first read, supporting cue, product or action read.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md, "pass 4 build the visual stop"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md#pass-4-build-the-visual-stop); [ad-creative/references/composition-review.md, "make and challenge the hierarchy"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/composition-review.md#make-and-challenge-the-hierarchy)
- **Check:** With the headline and sound taken away, would a distracted stranger have a specific reason to pause (or does the recorded opening dynamic's own test pass), and can the eye find the first read?
- **Evidence:** The frame description read alone with the copy hidden; the named reason to pause; the competing details removed, subordinated or kept on purpose.
- **If it fails:** A weak idea returns to premises or cast and treatment, never to headline polishing. Competing details are recomposed here.

### 5. Connected payoff (`connection`)

- **Build:** The hook as image, text and sound; then opening, development, turn, product payoff and action for video, or one complete proposition for a static; key copy and call to action.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md, "pass 5 connect opening turn payoff and action"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md#pass-5-connect-opening-turn-payoff-and-action)
- **Check:** Does the payoff answer the opening's promise with the product, so a viewer remembers the benefit and not only the opening?
- **Evidence:** The sequence or composition read start to end as a stranger would meet it.
- **If it fails:** A payoff that does not fulfil the opening returns to premises or the visual stop. Never attach a product screen to an unrelated opening.

### 6. Claims and permissions (`claims`)

- **Build:** The claims map: every factual thing the ad says, shows or implies (a number, rating, review, credential, guarantee, price, result, story, comparison, urgency, scarcity; implied claims count) set against the offer's proof, with the recorded permission behind any testimonial or customer story; then the policy check.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/SKILL.md, "2 run the claims gate before writing a word"](../core/skills/ad-creative/SKILL.md#2-run-the-claims-gate-before-writing-a-word); [ad-creative/SKILL.md, "3 run the policy and rules check"](../core/skills/ad-creative/SKILL.md#3-run-the-policy-and-rules-check); [ad-creative/references/claims-and-policy.md, "the gate"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/claims-and-policy.md#the-gate)
- **Check:** Does every claim the ad makes or implies map to a proof row or stand as a marked blank, with recorded permission behind every testimonial or customer story, and no personal attribute asserted?
- **Evidence:** The claims map read against the offer file's proof table, row by row; for each testimonial or story, the recorded permission; required strings read from the business's file.
- **If it fails:** An unsupported claim is cut or becomes a marked blank. A testimonial or story with no recorded permission is not used, and the reply says so in one line. A personal attribute is rewritten around the situation. An idea that only works with the unsupported claim returns to the connection layer.

### 7. Adversarial review and revision (`review`)

- **Build:** A separate review of each concept as a distracted viewer: attention, relevance and one job, promise and clarity, proof and policy, composition, distinctness and execution, brand continuity; rubric scores; revisions.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md, "pass 6 adversarial concept review then revision"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md#pass-6-adversarial-concept-review-then-revision); [ad-creative/references/copy-and-hooks.md, "the rubric"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/copy-and-hooks.md#the-rubric)
- **Check:** Does every gate pass with a concrete reason, with Hook and Glance at 4 or more and One job and Proof at 3 or more, and no average hiding a failed gate?
- **Evidence:** The seven verdicts with their reasons, the rubric scores, who reviewed (a fresh reviewer or self-review), and what changed in each revision cycle.
- **If it fails:** Return to the pass that failed, change that part, review the connected whole again. Three cycles at most, then withhold the concept and report the shortfall.

### 8. Script and sequence (`sequence`)

- **Build:** The sequence from Pass 5: opening, development, turn, product payoff, action; when the product enters and which question it answers; the hook as what is read, heard and in the first frame.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/video-build.md, "part 1 script and sequence"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/video-build.md#part-1-script-and-sequence)
- **Check:** Does the turn earn the reveal, with the product payoff connected to the opening rather than attached to an unrelated scene?
- **Evidence:** The sequence read start to end.
- **If it fails:** Return to the connection layer.

### 9. Copy per placement (`copy`)

- **Build:** The fields each placement needs, within the platform layer's lengths: hook as text, first frame and sound; primary text; headlines; description; call to action; required strings; the final claims map.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/SKILL.md, "4 develop and reject concepts then write the copy"](../core/skills/ad-creative/SKILL.md#4-develop-and-reject-concepts-then-write-the-copy); [ad-creative/references/copy-and-hooks.md, "the hook"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/copy-and-hooks.md#the-hook); [ad-creative/references/claims-and-policy.md, "the gate"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/claims-and-policy.md#the-gate)
- **Check:** Is every word a person will read true to the claims map, free of personal attributes, in the business's voice and the copy standard, and does the hook still carry the concept that passed?
- **Evidence:** Each field read against the claims map; a testimonial appears only where the claims layer recorded its permission; required strings read from the business's file; the rubric scores.
- **If it fails:** Rewrite the failing field here. A claim with no proof row is cut or marked blank. Copy that only works by changing the idea returns to the connection layer.

### 10. Ad against destination (`destination`)

- **Build:** The ad read beside the page it sends people to.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md, "pass 1 fundamentals before creative"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/creative-passes.md#pass-1-fundamentals-before-creative); [ad-creative/SKILL.md, "quality gate"](../core/skills/ad-creative/SKILL.md#quality-gate)
- **Check:** Does the page deliver what the ad promises, with the same offer and next action?
- **Evidence:** The destination page beside the ad, or the gap named.
- **If it fails:** A mismatch is fixed at the connection layer or reported to the business as a page gap. A page that cannot be seen from here is not checked: name it as an open item.

### 11. First frame (`first-frame`)

- **Build:** The actual first frame, described and composed.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/video-build.md, "part 2 first frame"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/video-build.md#part-2-first-frame)
- **Check:** Does this frame pass the Pass 4 test recorded at the visual-stop layer: the visual-alone stop test for a cold visual feed, or the selected opening dynamic's test?
- **Evidence:** The frame viewed, with the headline and sound taken away.
- **If it fails:** Recompose the frame, or return to the visual stop.

### 12. Scenes (`scenes`)

- **Build:** Supplied footage, generated scenes, or both, for the sequence.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/video-build.md, "part 3 scenes"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/video-build.md#part-3-scenes)
- **Check:** Do cast and treatment stay consistent, is the product demonstration true, and does generated imagery never misrepresent the product, the place or a person?
- **Evidence:** Each scene viewed, and the scenes compared with each other.
- **If it fails:** Remake the scene, or return to the concept if the idea cannot survive production.

### 13. Captions and sound together (`captions-sound`)

- **Build:** Burned-in captions and on-screen text, and the sound.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/video-build.md, "part 4 captions and sound together"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/video-build.md#part-4-captions-and-sound-together)
- **Check:** Does the ad read with the sound off, with text and logos inside the safe zones, generated text correct letter by letter and every claim identical to the gated draft?
- **Evidence:** The captions and on-screen text read against the gated copy; a play with the sound off.
- **If it fails:** Fix the caption, text or sound that is wrong.

### 14. The edit (`edit`)

- **Build:** The scenes assembled against the sequence.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/video-build.md, "part 5 the edit"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/video-build.md#part-5-the-edit)
- **Check:** Does the hook land in the first frame and first three seconds, with no competing movement or overlays in the frames and transitions, and the turn and payoff preserved?
- **Evidence:** The assembled cut played against the sequence.
- **If it fails:** Recut, or go back to the scene that causes it.

### 15. Placement versions (`versions`)

- **Build:** Only the authorised shapes; when a batch needs both feed and vertical, the vertical is a dedicated edit, not a crop.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/video-build.md, "part 6 placement versions"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/video-build.md#part-6-placement-versions)
- **Check:** In each authorised shape, is nothing cut off and the text inside that shape's safe zones?
- **Evidence:** Each version opened at its own size.
- **If it fails:** Recompose that version. A missing planned shape is named, not made beyond scope.

### 16. Watch the export (`watch`)

- **Build:** Each final file watched at the size it will be seen, and the gates re-run on the execution.
- **Method:** [ad-creative/references/video-build.md, "part 7 watch the export"](../core/skills/ad-creative/references/video-build.md#part-7-watch-the-export); [ad-creative/SKILL.md, "6 inspect the render"](../core/skills/ad-creative/SKILL.md#6-inspect-the-render)
- **Check:** Does each watched file hold its pacing, show only the gated claims and do the one job in the row?
- **Evidence:** The final files watched.
- **If it fails:** An unplayed file is unverified: say so and leave the row at copy-ready.

### Whole output (`whole`)

- **Method:** [ad-creative/SKILL.md, "quality gate"](../core/skills/ad-creative/SKILL.md#quality-gate)
- **Check:** Does the watched ad, with its copy in each placement, tell one connected story to the product and say only what the claims map allows?
- **Evidence:** Each exported version watched in full beside its copy.
- **If it fails:** A story that does not connect returns to sequence or connection. A version not watched keeps the row at copy-ready.

## When something changes

An old pass does not carry over. Check these again; leave unaffected work alone.

| If this changes | Check again | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A claim, the price, an offer term or a proof row changes | `claims`, `sequence`, `copy`, `destination`, `captions-sound`, `watch`, `whole`, work order [`launch`](launch.md) | Spoken lines, captions and frames that carry the old claim, and any approval that covered the old version. Scenes without the claim are kept. |
| The script or a line changes | `sequence`, `captions-sound`, `edit`, `watch`, `whole` | Scenes whose action did not change are kept. |
| A scene is replaced | `scenes`, `edit`, `watch`, `whole` | Check the cuts on both sides of it. |
| The person or product reference changes | `scenes`, `edit`, `versions`, `watch`, `whole` | Every scene that used the reference. |
| A placement shape is added | `versions`, `watch`, `whole` | Only the new shape. |
| The landing page changes | `fundamentals`, `destination` | The promise and the next action must still match. |

A layer in bold must be `pass`, or `carried` from an earlier record; `n/a` is not accepted there. The others may also be `n/a` with the reason. The whole-output row is never `n/a`, and no state stands on `n/a` rows alone. A layer outside the list that failed or was not checked is named beside the state. A state takes no exceptions (`rechecked, except the live ads`): claim the weaker state and name what remains.
